Maple Street Dental respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website or book an appointment, how we use that information, and the choices you have about it.

Information we collect

When you book an appointment we collect your name, email address, phone number and the reason for your visit, so that we can confirm the appointment and send you reminders. We do not sell this information to anyone.

Cookies and analytics

If you agree to it in our cookie banner, we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our website. If you choose "Reject all", no analytics cookies are set. You can change your choice at any time from the cookie settings link.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by contacting the practice. We will answer within thirty days.
