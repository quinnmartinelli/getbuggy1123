We fill in your forms but never send them, so no test messages reach your inbox. Security checks only look — they never try to break in — and a public report shows how many possible security problems we found, not the details. An automated accessibility scan catches only part of what a real visitor can run into. Cookie and privacy findings are general information, not legal advice.

Every report gets a score out of 100 (check again later to see if it went up), a link you can share, and a copy-and-paste version to send straight to your web person. Reports are deleted after 30 days.
