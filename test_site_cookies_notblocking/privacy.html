Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 3, 2019

Harbor Yoga Studio respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website or book a class, how we use that information, and the choices you have about it.

Information we collect

When you book a class we collect your name, email address and phone number so that we can confirm your booking and let you know about schedule changes. We do not sell this information to anyone.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our website, such as which pages are visited most often. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect this information in a form that does not directly identify you.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your booking details at any time by emailing the studio. You can also set your browser to refuse cookies, although some parts of the website may then not work as expected.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact the studio by email or visit us at the front desk during opening hours.
